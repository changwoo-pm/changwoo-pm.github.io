Grab · Executive alignment · Strategic planning

Connecting the investment to an execution roadmap

My role

Investment process leadership, cross-functional approval work, and partnership roadmap development.

Outcome

Executive approval secured

How I built the investment caseSimplified view of the workflow

Investment case → EV fleet collaboration → Future vehicle opportunities

Context

A strategic investment required agreement across finance, product, R&D, and legal, alongside a clear explanation of its value to Hyundai’s vehicle business.

Decision & trade-off

During executive review, Hyundai’s President asked how the investment would support the future of Hyundai’s vehicles. The financial case did not fully answer that question. I revised the proposal to make the connection explicit.

My actions

I organized the partnership around three stages: equity investment, EV fleet collaboration, and potential purpose-built vehicle development. I connected this roadmap to the business case and led the cross-functional approval work across finance, product, R&D, and legal.

Outcome

The revised case gained executive approval. It gave leadership a clearer connection between the investment and the proposed collaboration. EV fleet work followed; purpose-built vehicles remained a future direction.
